About the Institution

A Legacy of Engineering Excellence

Jayaraj Annapackiam CSI College of Engineering (JACSICOE) stands as a beacon of technical education in Southern Tamil Nadu. Located in the serene Margoschis Nagar, Nazareth, the institution is affiliated with Anna University and approved by AICTE.

Our mission is to nurture students into highly competent engineers who contribute meaningfully to society through innovation, research, and ethical practice. With state-of-the-art infrastructure and experienced faculty, we provide an environment where academic excellence thrives.

Computer Science Engineering

Master the art of software development, algorithms, data structures, and emerging technologies. Our CSE program prepares students for careers in software engineering, cloud computing, and cybersecurity with an industry-aligned curriculum.

MECH
Mechanical Engineering

Dive into the world of machines, thermodynamics, manufacturing, and design. Our program blends classical engineering principles with modern CAD/CAM tools, robotics, and sustainable manufacturing practices.
